Rules

Do and don’t

Do

  • Put the logo on cream or white. On Ink or Forest backgrounds, use the reversed horizontal or the badge (its cream ring keeps it separate).
  • Keep yellow to the daisy’s centre and small highlights.
  • Use Sage for leaves, soft shapes and quiet backgrounds.
  • Use the one-colour stamp when only one ink is allowed: embroidery, rubber stamps, vinyl, engraving.

Don’t

  • Don’t fill a background with yellow or set text in it on cream.
  • Don’t set text in Sage on cream; it’s too light to read.
  • Don’t recolour the dog or the leaves. He’s always cream; they’re always sage.
  • Don’t stretch, rotate or crop the circle, or move the daisy.
